## WE Invoice 0.1.6

eFaktúra for everyone, and more improvements from the first testers. An app that is already installed updates itself.

### New

- **eFaktúra is open to everyone:** no invitation code any more. Choose a certified provider in Settings, enter its key and send and receive eFaktúry. The feature stays marked Beta while it is new.
- **Company switcher on top:** with several companies, the switcher sits at the top of the menu under the logo; on a phone it is a strip over every screen.
- **Start screen:** while the app opens and syncs with Google Drive, a progress bar says what it is doing instead of an empty window.
- **Bills confirmed after their due date:** an old bill put in for the records asks whether it is paid already and offers the due date as the payment date, so it does not turn overdue by itself.

### Fixed

- The back arrow of a received invoice, an expense or an Inbox document goes back to the list again; it used to open the same document.
- After recording a payment the document shows it at once, and "Show all" in the change log removes the filter.
- The supplier's name read from a payment QR code is no longer marked "Check" when the document prints the same name, and a confirmed document shows no such marks.
- Electron and two libraries are updated to their latest patch versions.

### Installing

See [Installing](https://github.com/JaffarSk24/WE-Invoice#installing) in the README. The installers are not signed with a paid certificate yet: macOS needs one command after the first install, and Windows shows a SmartScreen warning.
