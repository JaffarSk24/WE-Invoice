## WE Invoice 0.1.1

A small update after the first public version. The desktop app installs it by itself.

### Fixed

- **Company look-up by IČO:** Slovak companies now come with their DIČ and IČ DPH from the open data of the Financial Administration, and the VAT payer reliability check answers. A company whose IČO also names branches closed long ago gets its current name instead of the name of an old branch.
- **E-mails in Czech, German, Russian and Ukrainian** keep the name eFaktúra in its basic form, as the app does.

### Installing

See [Installing](https://github.com/JaffarSk24/WE-Invoice#installing) in the README. The installers are not signed with a paid certificate yet: macOS needs one command after the first install, and Windows shows a SmartScreen warning. An app that is already installed updates itself.
