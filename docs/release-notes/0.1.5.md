## WE Invoice 0.1.5

Improvements from the first testers. An app that is already installed updates itself.

### New

- **Barcodes in the stock:** a receipt, issue or transfer starts with a Barcode or code field. Scan an item with a USB or Bluetooth scanner and it is picked, or a new item opens with the barcode already filled in. On a Mac and on Android the camera can read the barcode too.
- **Code and barcode of a new item** can be entered right when it first comes in with a receipt.
- **Favourites in the menu:** the star next to a section pins it on top of the menu.
- **New versions are hard to miss:** when an update is out, a large window shows what is new and updates the app in one click.

### Fixed

- The menu keeps its scroll position when you open a section.
- Primary buttons keep their colour when the pointer is over them; on the light theme their label used to disappear.

### Installing

See [Installing](https://github.com/JaffarSk24/WE-Invoice#installing) in the README. The installers are not signed with a paid certificate yet: macOS needs one command after the first install, and Windows shows a SmartScreen warning.
