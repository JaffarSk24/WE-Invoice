## WE Invoice 0.1.0

The first public version of WE Invoice: free invoicing and bookkeeping for Slovak businesses, ready for eFaktúra from 1 January 2027. Your data, attachment files included, stays encrypted in your own Google Drive; White Eagles runs no data servers.

### What you can do

- **Invoices and other documents:** invoices, proforma invoices, tax documents for received payments, credit and debit notes, quotes, orders, delivery notes and quick sales, with conversions between them and file attachments. The particulars of § 74 of the VAT act are checked before a document is issued.
- **PDF and QR payments:** four templates with your logo, stamp and signature, printing in Slovak, Czech, English, German or two languages at once, PAY by square on every invoice.
- **Clients and prices:** clients from the business register by IČO, VIES and VAT payer reliability checks, price lists with price levels, recurring invoices and payment reminders sent on their day.
- **Bank and cash:** statement import from Slovak and Czech banks (CSV, CAMT.053, MT940, ABO/GPC) with automatic matching, SEPA payment orders, cash book and set-off.
- **Purchases:** received invoices and expenses from PDF, QR codes, photos (local OCR) and eKasa receipts.
- **eFaktúra (beta):** sending and receiving through the certified provider you choose, in the Peppol BIS 3.0 format with the Slovak rules; the attachments of a document travel inside the e-invoice.
- **VAT and bookkeeping:** VAT records, Kontrolný výkaz, Súhrnný výkaz and Priznanie k DPH as XML that validates against the official schemas, bookkeeping for sole traders with fixed assets and the year-end closing. The tax reports are being checked by an accountant; check them before you file.
- **Stock and reports:** warehouses and stock moves from documents, sales and cash flow reports, ageing of receivables, global search, recycle bin and change log.
- **For your accountant:** exports for Pohoda, Money S3, Omega, ISDOC and XLSX in one ZIP, migration from other invoicing apps (SuperFaktúra also through its API, Shoptet orders, old .xls files), a full backup with a password.
- **Everywhere:** desktop app for macOS and Windows with a tray icon and automatic updates, and a web app that installs on iPhone and Android and works offline. The interface speaks Slovak, Czech, English, German, Russian and Ukrainian.

### Installing

See [Installing](https://github.com/JaffarSk24/WE-Invoice#installing) in the README. The installers are not signed with a paid certificate yet: macOS needs one command after the first install, and Windows shows a SmartScreen warning.
