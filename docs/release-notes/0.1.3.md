## WE Invoice 0.1.3

Copying and deleting invoices, editable numbers and a cleaner layout. An app that is already installed updates itself.

### New

- **Copy an invoice from the list:** select one document and choose Copy. A draft with the same content opens for editing; it gets the next number of its series when you issue it.
- **Edit the number of an issued invoice** in its editor. A number another document of the series already has is refused, and a variable symbol made from the number follows the new one.
- **Delete an issued invoice** from its card (More, Delete). When it had the last number of its series, that number goes to the next document. An invoice sent as an e-invoice, locked after an export to the accountant or in a closed VAT period cannot be deleted.

### Fixed

- **PDF:** the payment QR sits in the middle above "PAY by square", and an invoice of a seller who is not a VAT payer no longer prints a VAT summary.
- **Layout across the app:** cards of one row line up, narrow columns no longer break words letter by letter, table headers cover every column, settings, the quick sale and the dashboard fit tablets, phone screens no longer scroll sideways, and buttons and menus keep their labels on one line. The contact card shows its terms in a full-width row.

### Installing

See [Installing](https://github.com/JaffarSk24/WE-Invoice#installing) in the README. The installers are not signed with a paid certificate yet: macOS needs one command after the first install, and Windows shows a SmartScreen warning.
