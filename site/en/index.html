E-invoicing from 1 January 2027

From 1 January 2027, invoices between businesses go electronic

Act No. 385/2025 Coll. makes e-invoicing (eFaktúra) via the Peppol network mandatory. And not just for large companies.

Who has to issue e-invoices

VAT payers, for every domestic invoice to another business or public body. The deadline for issuing is 15 days from delivery.

Who has to receive e-invoices

Every business and legal entity in Slovakia, including sole traders who are not VAT payers. There is no exemption from receiving.

What stays the same

Invoices to consumers, simplified eKasa receipts up to 400 € and tax-exempt supplies remain as they are.

WE Invoice helps you both receive and send e-invoices. A received invoice is imported in full, every line included, and a sent one is kept in its original XML for 10 years, as the law requires.

What WE Invoice does

Everything in one app, with no paid add-ons and no Premium tier.

Invoices and documents

Invoices, proforma invoices, credit notes, quotes, delivery notes and sales receipts. PDFs with a PAY by square QR code, printed in Slovak, Czech, English, German or bilingually.

E-invoicing (Peppol)

Send and receive through a provider you choose yourself. A received invoice is imported in full, a sent one is archived in its original XML.

Bank and payment matching

Import statements from any bank for any period. Payments are matched to invoices by variable symbol, amount and customer.

Payment reminders with interest

Three reminder levels, statutory late-payment interest and the flat 40 € recovery charge. Sent by e-mail, even when you don't have the app open.

Expenses and receipt scanning

Snap a receipt or scan the eKasa QR code and the expense is recorded. Recognition runs on your device, free and without limits.

VAT filings

VAT control statement, EC sales list and VAT return as XML, ready to file through the Financial Administration (financnasprava.sk) portal.

Single-entry bookkeeping

Cash journal, receivables and payables ledger and the figures for a sole trader's tax return. Or just the records you need for flat-rate expenses.

Several companies, free

As many companies as you want. No extra charge for another company, another user or another document.

Phone and desktop

The web app runs in a browser and on your phone; the desktop version for macOS and Windows works offline and sends recurring invoices in the background.

How to switch on e-invoicing in three steps

WE Invoice is not an e-invoicing provider. It's a client for the provider you choose yourself. You never pay twice.

  1. Choose an e-invoicing provider (digitálny poštár)

    A certified e-invoicing service provider. Receiving is free with several of them; sending costs from 0 to about 3 € a month.

  2. Register your provider with the Financial Administration

    Log in to the Financial Administration (financnasprava.sk) with your chip-enabled ID card and confirm your provider.

  3. Paste the API key into WE Invoice

    Settings, E-invoicing, provider and key. From then on, invoices are sent and received straight from the app.

You can switch providers at any time. Your invoices and XML archive stay with you.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free? Why?

Yes: no plans, no limits, no trial period. We have almost no running costs: the site runs on GitHub Pages, your data is in your Google Drive and you pay for e-invoicing directly to your provider. The app is built by the White Eagles agency as a showcase of its work and a way to reach small business owners.

Where is my data?

On your device and in your Google Drive, encrypted with a key only you know. Our server is just a gateway to e-invoicing providers, public registers and exchange rates: it stores nothing. In the web version your provider API key also passes through it, because providers don't accept requests straight from a browser; the gateway hands it over only for the duration of the request and never logs or caches it. The desktop version for macOS and Windows talks to the provider directly from your computer and bypasses the gateway; if you want maximum privacy, choose that one.

What if I lose my password?

On first launch you get a recovery key. Keep it away from your computer, printed out for example. Without the password and the recovery key the data cannot be decrypted; not even we can do it.

Do I have to receive e-invoices even if I'm not a VAT payer?

Yes. From 1 January 2027 every business and every legal entity in Slovakia has to receive e-invoices. Only VAT payers have to issue them. Receiving is free with several providers.

How much does an e-invoicing provider cost?

Several providers offer receiving for free. Sending costs from 0 to about 3 € a month, or a few cents per document, depending on the provider. You pay them directly; WE Invoice adds nothing on top.

Why does my Mac say the developer is unverified?

The desktop app isn't yet signed with an Apple developer certificate, which costs 99 USD a year. On first launch, right-click the app and choose Open, or allow it under Settings, Privacy & Security. Later updates run without the warning.

Will it replace my accountant?

No. It prepares the groundwork: reports, journal, exports in common formats. You or your accountant remain responsible for the accuracy of tax documents and for filing them. A good accountant saves time with WE Invoice; the work is still theirs.

What we don't do, and why

No eKasa cash register of our own and no direct link to the VRP virtual register: Act No. 384/2025 Coll. bans using VRP through third-party apps, and a cash register of our own would need Financial Administration certification. When an invoice is paid in cash, we prepare the data for VRP and store the receipt number. No real-time bank connection via PSD2: it needs a licence and your data would pass through someone else's server, so we import statements instead. No SMS reminders: a paid channel we would have to charge for. E-mail and Peppol instead.
